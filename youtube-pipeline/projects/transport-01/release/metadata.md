Your sneakers may well have crossed the Pacific in a sealed steel box, on a ship run by about twenty people.
We follow one 40-foot container from a factory near Shanghai to your front porch, with every handoff and the real numbers.

In this video:
- What a TEU is, and why the 40-foot box counts as two
- Shanghai, the world's busiest container port (55 million TEU in 2025)
- Giant ships like the MSC Irina, tiny crews and slow steaming
- Why the route bends toward Alaska, and how many containers really fall overboard
- Los Angeles, the 2022 ship backlog and the radiation check on the way out
- Double-stack trains to Chicago in about three days
- Distribution centers, the last mile, and the empty trip back
- Malcolm McLean, the Ideal X, and the 1956 number that made all of this cheap
