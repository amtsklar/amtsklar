One AI text prompt uses about as much energy as nine seconds of TV. So why did data center electricity use jump almost 20% worldwide in 2025?
We follow the power from the high-voltage line, through the substation and backup batteries, into a 120-kilowatt AI rack, and out the other side as heat.

In this video:
- How much electricity data centers really use (US and worldwide)
- From transmission line to chip: substations, UPS batteries, diesel generators
- Why an AI rack draws about 15x the power of an average rack
- Where the power goes: heat, air vs. liquid cooling, water use
- PUE, the one number that shows how wasteful a data center is
- Why demand keeps climbing even as AI gets more efficient
- Three Mile Island, small modular reactors and the hunt for power

This channel explains how energy and digital infrastructure work. It is not financial or investment advice.
